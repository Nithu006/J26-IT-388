# Privacy and ethics requirements

Source: guide pp. 9–10. This is a requirements record, not a legal or ethics approval.

Before processing real records, confirm permitted research use, applicable platform
terms and university clearance. Public visibility alone does not establish permission.
Remove direct identifiers, review text for identifying content, keep any linkage keys
separate and use pseudonymous groups only for leakage prevention.

Keep raw data, models, local configurations and reports outside version control.
Repository ignore rules do not provide encryption, access controls or deletion.
The data custodian must define restricted storage, retention/deletion, backups and
authorized users before introducing real data. Aggregate rare-class counts may be sensitive.
The audit never prints record previews, but reports require access review.

Future inference must avoid storing/logging sensitive text and identities. Restrict
hosted access and use appropriate deployment controls. Display research-only notices;
do not claim diagnosis or automate consequential decisions about people. Crisis
intervention and treatment recommendations are outside this component.
