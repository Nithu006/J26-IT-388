Limitations (say these out loud)

  • Small sample (see Overview) — results are preliminary, with wide variance.
  • Labels are dataset-provided/self-reported, not clinical.
  • Night ratio is relative: timestamps carry mixed UTC offsets, local time zone unknown.
  • No true engagement data (likes/replies) — @mentions and post length are proxies.
  • Risk thresholds in the demo are illustrative, not clinically validated.
  • Merged datasets use different labels: Thai = depression, Twitter-STMHD = any of 8 disorders vs control, Reddit = self-reported identity claim (all positive, no controls, no text → length/mentions are 0).
  • Next: full Twitter-STMHD, Transformer comparison, fusion with text & image components.
